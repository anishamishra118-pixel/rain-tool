Rain Drops

Generative particle studies of a raindrop's life. Each piece as the original HTML tool and recreated in p5.js. Press H in any piece to hide its controls.

  1. Snow drop
    Originalp5.js
    Snowflake arms grow out from the ring; crystals grow inward to a hollow.
  2. Sleet drop
    Originalp5.js
    Refreezing from the outside in: ice shell, shrinking core, spikes, cracks.
  3. Land drop
    Originalp5.js
    Reaching land: splash crown, spray, soak-in stain, petrichor.
  4. Frozen living drop
    Originalp5.js
    A solid drop that keeps moving; crisp edges and soft, creeping edges.
  5. Pressure drop
    Originalp5.js
    Under pressure and resistance: stretched to a distorted U, then eased back.
  6. Untouched drop
    Originalp5.js
    Untouched by air pressure or resistance: a barely breathing ring and haze.
  7. Thaw drop
    Originalp5.js
    Frozen → Thaw → Split, on one slider. Slice the thawed ring with the cursor.
